Free · Private Chrome history search

Find anything you browsed. Understand where your time went.

Better Browser History makes your History page searchable by page content, groups visits into sessions and shows measured foreground-time insights—all stored locally on your device.

Get the latest release How it works

No account · No ads · No analytics SDK · Capture is off until you enable it

A history that helps you get back to work

Search by what was on a page, not only by the words you remember from its title. Then use sessions, journeys and time insights to understand your browsing.

Full-text history search

Find saved pages using their title, URL or visible page text, with deterministic local ranking.

Time analytics

See measured active-time shares, daily trends, busy hours, top sites and measurement coverage.

Sessions and journeys

Reconnect related visits into browsing sessions and navigation paths instead of one endless list.

Notes and organization

Star important pages, add tags and notes, filter repeat visits, and export or import a JSON backup.

Private by design

The extension explains what it will collect before capture begins. If you enable it, information is written to the extension's local IndexedDB database and used only for its visible history features.

Your browser stays yours.Better Browser History replaces only Chrome's History page. It does not replace New Tab, your homepage, startup pages, bookmarks or search provider.

Open source and inspectable

There are no remote scripts and no server dependency. The capture pipeline, deterministic analytics, automated tests and packaged releases are available in the public repository.
