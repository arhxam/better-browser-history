## Product name

**Better Browser History**

## Summary

**Search and analyze browser history with full-text search, sessions and time insights—all stored locally on your device.**

The summary is 117 characters and stays below Chrome's 132-character limit.

## Detailed description

Paste the following text exactly:

> Better Browser History turns Chrome's History page into a private, searchable browser history manager with full-text search and time-based insights.
>
> Find a page even when you remember its content rather than its title. Review repeat visits, organize useful pages with tags, notes and stars, follow browsing sessions and navigation journeys, and see where your measured foreground time goes.
>
> FEATURES
>
> • Full-text browser history search across saved titles, URLs and visible page text  
> • Active-time analytics by site and category, plus daily trends and an hour-by-weekday heatmap  
> • Sessions and navigation journeys reconstructed from visits  
> • Repeat-visit counts, stars, tags, notes and filters  
> • Local JSON export and import  
> • Configurable site exclusions, capture controls and retention  
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
>
> • Capture is off until you review the disclosure and explicitly enable it  
> • History data is stored locally in your browser profile  
> • No account, cloud history service, ads or analytics SDK  
> • Pause capture, exclude sites, disable content or engagement capture, or clear everything at any time  
> • No remotely hosted executable code  
>
> Better Browser History replaces only Chrome's History page. It does not replace New Tab, your homepage, startup pages, bookmarks or search provider.
>
> Chrome does not allow extensions to inspect protected pages such as chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store. Active-time figures cover measurable foreground time and clearly show their measurement coverage.

## Search positioning

Primary intent: **browser history search**. Supporting user intents are **Chrome history
analytics**, **time spent by website**, **full-text browsing history**, and **private
local browser history**. These phrases already appear naturally in the title, summary,
description, website headings and documentation. Do not append a keyword block or repeat
any phrase unnaturally.

Sustainable Store ranking depends on listing relevance plus product quality signals:
successful installs, continued use, low uninstall rates and genuine ratings. The launch
therefore prioritizes a truthful listing, useful onboarding, reliable capture, fast local
search, public support and explicit privacy controls over keyword density.
